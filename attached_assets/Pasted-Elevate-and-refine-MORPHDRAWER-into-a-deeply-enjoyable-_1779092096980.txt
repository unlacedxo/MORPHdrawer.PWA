Elevate and refine MORPHDRAWER into a deeply enjoyable beginner drawing experience focused on creativity, confidence, emotional immersion, and playful learning.

Do NOT redesign the app from scratch.

Do NOT add unnecessary complexity.

Instead:

* deepen the atmosphere
* strengthen the beginner experience
* enrich the sketchbook immersion
* improve visual clarity
* increase emotional attachment
* make drawing feel approachable and rewarding

The app should feel like:
a living sketchbook companion that gently teaches users how to enjoy drawing strange lovable things.

⸻

BEGINNER EXPERIENCE IS THE TOP PRIORITY

Every design decision should support:

* lowering drawing anxiety
* encouraging experimentation
* making creativity feel playful
* helping users start drawing immediately
* rewarding imperfect sketching
* making users emotionally attached to their creations

The app should NEVER feel:

* intimidating
* overly technical
* academically artistic
* professionally demanding
* cluttered
* overwhelming

The experience should feel:
“wait… I can actually draw this.”

MAKE OUTPUTS MORE DRAWABLE

Strengthen the creature generation system so forms feel:

* instantly visualizable
* silhouette-driven
* memorable
* simple to sketch
* emotionally expressive

Prioritize:

* chunky + spiky
* floppy + round
* crooked + tiny
* stretched + soft
* lumpy + angular
* blob-like + thorny

Avoid:

* overly abstract wording
* complicated anatomy
* long descriptive paragraphs
* dense lore

* tiny silhouette previews
