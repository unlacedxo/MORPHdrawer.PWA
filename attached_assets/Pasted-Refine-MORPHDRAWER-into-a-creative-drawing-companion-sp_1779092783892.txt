Refine MORPHDRAWER into a creative drawing companion specifically designed to help users learn drawing fundamentals through playful creature design and sketchbook experimentation.

The primary purpose of the app is:
- helping users practice drawing
- improving visual thinking
- building silhouette understanding
- encouraging experimentation
- making drawing feel fun and emotionally rewarding

Users will take the generated ideas into Procreate and sketch them manually.

The app should support:
- creativity
- confidence
- exploration
- visual imagination
- shape language learning

NOT:
- realism
- hyper-detail
- complicated lore
- professional concept art workflows
- intimidating art instruction

--------------------------------------------------
CORE DRAWING PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------

The app should teach drawing indirectly through play.

Users should naturally improve at:
- silhouettes
- proportions
- exaggeration
- visual balance
- shape combinations
- readable designs
- emotional expression
- feature hierarchy

without feeling like they are “studying.”

The experience should feel:
creative, cozy, weird, playful, and freeing.

--------------------------------------------------
MAKE SHAPES THE MAIN FOCUS
--------------------------------------------------

Every generated creature should prioritize:
- silhouette readability
- strong shape language
- memorable forms
- easy sketch construction

Use combined shape traits:
- chunky + spiky
- floppy + stretched
- tiny + jagged
- lumpy + round
- crooked + soft
- angular + blob-like

The silhouette should be understandable in seconds.

--------------------------------------------------
ADD VISUAL DRAWING AIDS
--------------------------------------------------

Every generated form should include:
- a tiny silhouette icon
- rough doodled shape preview
- simplified blob version
- visual shape hints

NOT polished art.

The goal is:
helping users mentally “see” the drawing immediately.

--------------------------------------------------
MAKE DRAWING TIPS PRACTICAL
--------------------------------------------------

Avoid advanced art advice.

Do NOT teach:
- rendering
- anatomy
- perspective theory
- lighting systems

Instead focus on:
- shape construction
- exaggeration
- simplification
- visual rhythm
- silhouette flow

Good examples:
- start with one big circle
- make the eyes oversized
- keep the limbs tiny
- draw the silhouette first
- keep one side uneven
- make the shape wobble slightly
- add dangling details last

The app should constantly reduce fear and perfectionism.

--------------------------------------------------
ENCOURAGE ITERATION
--------------------------------------------------

The app should encourage users to:
- redraw creatures multiple times
- evolve old sketches
- experiment with proportions
- remix silhouettes
- push shapes further
- exaggerate features

The notebook should feel like:
a playground for visual experimentation.

--------------------------------------------------
MAKE EVOLUTION A DRAWING EXERCISE
--------------------------------------------------

Evolution should teach visual iteration.

Each evolution should:
- alter silhouettes
- exaggerate features
- shift proportions
- mutate shapes
- introduce asymmetry
- increase visual personality

Examples:
- eyes multiply
- sleeves become wings
- body stretches taller
- shape softens
- limbs shrink
- silhouette becomes sharper

Users should feel like:
they are practicing visual design naturally.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEPEN THE SKETCHBOOK FEEL
--------------------------------------------------

The notebook atmosphere should support creativity and inspiration.

Add subtle:
- taped corners
- sketch marks
- graphite smudges
- notebook arrows
- page wear
- tiny doodles
- silhouette stamps
- erased construction marks
- recurring creature motifs

The app should feel:
used, loved, messy, artistic, and emotionally alive.

--------------------------------------------------
PROTECT THE CORE IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT turn MORPHDRAWER into:
- a realistic AI art generator
- a chatbot
- a productivity app
- a complex art education platform
- a professional concept art suite

The strength of MORPHDRAWER is:
- playful drawing inspiration
- beginner accessibility
- shape experimentation
- emotional sketchbook immersion
- weird lovable forms
- visual creativity practice

The app should feel like:
a living sketchbook companion that helps users fall in love with drawing weird little creatures in Procreate.
